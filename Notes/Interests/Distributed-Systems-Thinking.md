## TLA+ (Temporal Logic of Actions +)
*(You mentioned TPA+ — this is almost certainly TLA+, the industry-standard formal spec language for distributed/concurrent systems. Super common mix-up!)*

- Created by **Leslie Lamport** (Turing Award winner, Paxos, etc.).
- Used heavily at AWS (S3, DynamoDB, EBS, etc.) for verifying complex distributed algorithms.
- Helps you reason about **invariants**, model-check protocols, and avoid the usual nightmares of concurrency and failure modes.
